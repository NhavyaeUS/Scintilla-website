why scintilla?

The
Spark

Scintilla means “spark” — and also happens to be the name of the thing you’re looking at right now.

It started with a simple observation: a lot of genuinely good writing, art, and half-finished poems were just sitting in people’s notes apps, going nowhere. Scintilla exists to change that — a space built by students, for students, to actually be read. No gatekeeping, no “is this good enough” spiral.

We’re entirely student-run, from the writing to the design to the late-night debates over fonts. What you see here is the result of that effort — a platform for the voices of Shiv Nadar University Chennai to be heard beyond a group chat.

Est. 2024 · Student-run · Caffeine-powered
PREVIOUS EDITION

Making a magazine from scratch is easy, said no one on this team, ever.

Our debut issue took months, a lot of caffeine, and more revisions than we’re willing to admit. There was a group chat that never slept, a submissions inbox that somehow kept surprising us, and at least one night where “we’ll never finish this” was said out loud and meant.

But it’s out. It’s real. And it’s got some genuinely great work in it from people across campus who trusted us with their writing, their art, their weird 2am ideas. Go read it — we promise it was worth the chaos.
